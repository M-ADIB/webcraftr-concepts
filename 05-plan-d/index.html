(06) Questions

Questions before you start

What is the free 1:1 call?

A free first conversation with Diana. You talk it through, and afterwards you decide whether to go further.

Who is Plan D for?

Adults who feel overwhelmed, stretched thin or burnt out and want a lifestyle reset that covers food and daily life.

How is the Signature Program different from the other services?

Nutrition Coaching, Life Coaching and Fitness Coaching are each available on their own. The Signature Program blends nutrition and life coaching into one complete lifestyle reset.

Does Plan D replace medical care?

Plan D offers coaching and nutrition guidance. It does not replace medical care. If you manage a medical condition, mention it on your call.
