(01) Stance

Dubai property strategy

The entry price is the loudest number in Dubai property. The exit is the one that decides the result.

  1. No yield promises.

    I publish no return or yield figures. Numbers are discussed privately, once your profile is clear.

  2. No launch countdowns.

    No urgency and no last units. A launch date is not a reason to buy.

  3. No listings before the plan.

    Strategy comes first. I discuss specific properties only once the right permits and approvals are in place.
