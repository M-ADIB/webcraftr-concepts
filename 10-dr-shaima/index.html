Stage 1 of 7

The first tooth

The first tooth often appears around 6 months. Many dentists suggest a first visit around the first tooth.

Stage 2 of 7

Toddler years

More baby teeth come in. Feeding and soothing habits can affect them, so this is a good time to ask what to watch for.

Stage 3 of 7

Preschool years

By about age 3, most children have all 20 baby teeth. Visits at this age are often about getting comfortable, so tell the front desk if your child is nervous.

Stage 4 of 7

Baby teeth start to loosen

Around age 6, the first adult molars arrive and baby teeth begin to loosen. A good time to ask how the new teeth are coming in.

Stage 5 of 7

Mixed teeth

Baby and adult teeth sit side by side. This is when many families start asking about crowding and braces.

Stage 6 of 7

Early teens

Most adult teeth are in by now. Teens can ask about braces and clear aligners such as Invisalign, which Dr Shaima offers.

Stage 7 of 7

Late teens

Teens can start to take charge of their own visits. Bring any questions about braces, aligners or daily care.
